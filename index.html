📊 Scoring System

  • Group Winner: 5 pts
  • Group winner is one of your 4 contenders: 2 pts
  • Best In Show (Exact Match): 20 pts
  • Best In Show was one of your group picks: 5 pts
Tiebreaker

If two ballots have the same points, check each one's Best in Show pick. If that dog won its group (so it made the final 7), that ballot moves ahead.

Example: Amy and Bob both have 30 points. Amy picked the Pug for Best in Show, and the Pug won the Toy Group. Bob picked the Akita, which didn't win the Working Group. Amy ranks ahead of Bob.
